The problem

Concussion care is a guess at both ends.

The decisions that matter most still rest on what an injured athlete says about themselves.

Detection

Self-reported and easy to game.

Sideline protocols lean on symptom reports that athletes have every reason to underplay when they want to stay on the field.

Clearance

The same checklist, at the riskiest moment.

Return to play is decided by the same subjective process, exactly when second-impact risk and institutional liability are highest.

How it works

One engine. Three moments.

Voice can be captured anywhere, on a phone, in under a minute, at almost no marginal cost. No blood draw, no EEG cap, no lab.

01

Baseline

Each athlete records once, at preseason or intake. Every later result is compared with their own voice, not a population average.

02

Screen

After a knock, a 20-second sideline sample returns Clear, Watch or Refer against that baseline.

03

Monitor

Daily 20-second check-ins from home build an objective recovery curve through return to play and return to work.

Signals

Voice first. More of the brain's signals next.

Every other concussion signal is a snapshot: invasive, hardware-bound or expensive to repeat. Voice is the one you can take every day. The same engine will add eye tracking and other measures as they're validated.
